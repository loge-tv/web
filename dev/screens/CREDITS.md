# Credits for the snapshot baselines

The pictures in `baseline/` show Loge Web running a test library of open movies by the Blender
Foundation / Blender Institute / Blender Studio. Their posters, backdrops and stills come from the
sources below, under the licences those sources state. Changes made: the pictures were cropped and
scaled.

The library, the server and the screen states are a test setup, not a real user's Plex server. In it
Sprite Fright appears as a show of three episodes, although the film is a single short. The player's
picture is hidden in the baselines. The trailer dialog shows a local stand-in, not YouTube.

Film titles and logos on the posters, and the Blender logo, are trademarks of their owners. The
Blender Studio licensing pages exclude logos and trademarks from the Creative Commons licence.
Blender Studio's terms say: "Unless notified otherwise, all digital content (webpages, video,
artwork, 3D data) is available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0"
